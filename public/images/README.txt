IMAGES REQUIRED
===============

Please add these images to this folder before deploying:

1. advocate-pavan-rathod.jpg  (Professional headshot, 400x400px minimum)
2. og-image.jpg               (Social share image, 1200x630px)
3. logo.png                   (Logo if available)

For case study images, add:
- cases/family-law.jpg
- cases/criminal-law.jpg
- cases/civil-law.jpg
- cases/business-law.jpg
- cases/property-law.jpg

Gallery images are loaded from Unsplash CDN by default.
Replace the URLs in src/data/gallery.json with your actual image paths.
